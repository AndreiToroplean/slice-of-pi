Try it

Type π from memory on the phone (your keyboard works too). A wrong digit, or End run, saves the run to the stats page. "Watch a run" plays 75 digits as a made-up player who groups mostly in threes, in real time.

Palette: 10 colors, in the order every key steps through them

What's in this version

  • Key colors come from π (colors.md): each digit key shows, as an accent, the color its digit will get next. Pressing it fills the key with that color at once, and on release the key shows its next color in the band at its bottom. With 10 colors for 10 keys, each key starts on its own color. No seed is involved, so everyone gets the same colors.
  • Typed groups keep their slots, joined into one word with only its outer corners rounded, and the slots fly up to the typed digits in that form.
  • The palette has 10 colors. Every key steps through the same palette, so the keypad drifts around the color wheel as you go.
  • No text under the slots and no season banner. The season header and year bar stay as they were.
  • Groups are unbounded. Type past the hint and a slot is added per digit; the row grows to 10, then a new row starts below.
  • Groups on screen follow the aggregate, current run included: a group closes at a confident aggregate break, or when your wait is long enough to make the aggregate break there. For a new player that's just your pace in this run (local pace plus a fraction of its standard deviation; a fixed 0.9 s while there are fewer than three intervals).
  • Slots follow the aggregate where it's confident enough, and show 5 everywhere else, as for a new player.
Stats page (technical)

Runs, breaks and the aggregate

Each run is a chart of the time per digit (the inverse of speed, so pauses are peaks) against the digit number. Red bars are the breaks found after the run, fainter where the confidence is low. The four example runs are made up; your own runs are added under them and kept in this browser.
