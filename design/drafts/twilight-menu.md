# Twilight main menu (draft)

A step toward the lake redesign ([lake-handoff.md](lake-handoff.md)), not the whole of it: the main menu takes on the redesign's look first, and the rest of the game follows later.

## The mood

- The game's world is a starry night, toward sunset: its main views are a night sky full of stars, or a lake at twilight. Colors and mood come from there.
- The buttons stay colorful, the key colors kept as they are. Between us, those colors are lanterns: the words on the tape, for instance, could become lanterns one day.
- The game runs full screen, and the color around it (the browser's bars, the splash screen, the surroundings on wide screens) is the deep blue of a sky at twilight rather than pink.

## The main menu

- The menu is the sky alone, as if the camera had panned up from the play screen, whose horizon sits about halfway down: deep blue at the top, the last of the day's light at the bottom, just above a horizon we don't see.
- Tapping Play pans the camera down: the horizon and the lake come into view, and the game starts.
- Until the lake redesign, the menu reuses the season background's gradient with its own twilight colors, rather than a new mechanism.
- The game's icon stands in the sky with no background of its own.
- The title's letters are lanterns, each in a key color, giving off colored light with irregular rays. The icon is lit from behind, as by a warm source of light.
- Light follows depth: "Pi" is nearest, then "Slice of", then the icon. Each one's light falls on everything behind it, as well as on the sky.
